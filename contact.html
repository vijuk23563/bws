❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free home visits?

Yes! We offer a free home visit for measurement and consultation. Book your slot on our booking page.

How long does a custom order take?

Depending on the complexity, standard orders take 3–6 weeks. Rush orders can be done in 2 weeks with prior arrangement.

Do you provide installation?

Yes, we handle delivery and installation at your location. This is included in the project cost.

Is there a warranty on your products?

All our products come with a 1-year craftsmanship warranty. We stand behind the quality of our work.
